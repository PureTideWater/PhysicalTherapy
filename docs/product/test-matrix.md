## Mapping and coverage (MAP)

| ID | Case | Assertion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MAP-01 | Unmapped payer and plan | No line of the plan is ever priced; a line the resolver reaches is left unpriced as `NO_MAPPING`, one by one; the run prices every other line of its period. Coverage shows dollars waiting |
| MAP-02 | Mapping with an effective date after some lines | Earlier lines `NO_MAPPING`; later priced |
| MAP-03 | Mapping corrected | New mapping; old run unchanged; re-run offered |
| MAP-04 | Mapped as no agreement | Lines go to anomaly screening only |
| MAP-05 | Mapped as MA non-contracted | Priced from the fee schedule with all flags on |
| MAP-06 | A month holding lines of a mapped plan and of a plan with no mapping | Priced by the monitor and by a person's run, in the worker and with nothing refused in Postgres: the mapped plan's lines price under their mapping, and no line of the other plan is priced (`NO_MAPPING` one by one where the resolver reaches it; `ENGINE_ERROR` for a line that fails the engine's fact checks first). The run's coverage records the plan with no mapping, all its lines and billed cents, equal to what Coverage counts for that month. Coverage, Pricing runs and the home page say the plan has no mapping and its lines are not priced; the run's page and the printed audit report say it had no mapping when the run was priced, and the printed coverage report that it had none when the report was made, in counts and cents only. The engine is unchanged |
| MAP-07 | A period none of whose plans has a mapping, and the mapping that completes it | No run is created: the monitor leaves the month waiting, logged as `UNMAPPED_PLAN` with the count of months, and a person's run is refused as pricing nothing. A mapping queues monitoring only when, with a release in force, it leaves a month no run covers with every plan of its lines mapped and no wait on the billing ZIP or on Medicare content holding it; that job names every such month, by its first day only, and prices those months alone as first runs in effect with no hand re-run, never a month still waiting on another plan's mapping (with month 1 holding plans A and B and month 2 holding A and C, mapping A, then B, then C gives each month one first run and no re-run). A mapping that leaves the month mixed, or held on the billing ZIP, queues nothing and says only "Mapped", and the page says how many months wait and offers "Price them now", which queues monitoring of every uncovered month and is refused while the practice is suspended. Two people mapping a month's last two plans at the same moment queue monitoring exactly once. A month whose last run waited on Medicare content is planned again once a mapping completes it |
| MAP-08 | Plans mapped after their months were priced | The mapping list and each plan's page list, oldest first, each run in effect holding `NO_MAPPING` lines that a mapping now covers, with their lines and billed dollars. "Price these months again" creates one re-run of each in that person's name, queued and not accepted, with one audit event per re-run; two plans mapped and pressed in turn give one counting re-run per month, and a second press of the same page, by anyone, at any moment, including two presses at once, creates no second re-run. A month whose first run of overlapping dates is queued or being priced is not re-run (`newer_run_pending`) and says it waits. A month whose re-run since the mapping is pending links to it; one whose re-run is superseded or pinned to a withdrawn release is ready again. A month whose re-run since the mapping failed on the billing ZIP or on Medicare content says so, links to it and is not offered again until the location is saved, the terms change or a release loads more of that content; after any other failure it is ready again and says the last re-run failed. Once accepted, the month says to triage it on Cases until triage has validated its short lines that were `NO_MAPPING` before; a re-run accepted after a sign-off or a release alone is not listed. A job never creates the re-runs |
| MAP-09 | Pricing months again from a page that is out of date, or without the right | When a month became ready that the form did not send, or the form sent a month that is neither ready nor already being priced again, the request is refused as `months_changed` with nothing created, the refused field is named in the form's summary, and the page shows the months as they are now. A refusal of one month names it by its dates and creates nothing. A run of another practice, a plan of another practice, a release not in force, a practice that is suspended, and a person without `mapping.edit` are refused, with nothing created |

| PRC-32 | A run's cut-off and creation, to the microsecond | A service line whose created_at equals the run's own created_at (loaded at the instant of the transaction that creates the run) is the run's at creation and in the price job alike: its period is not refused as holding no service line, nor as nothing mapped (MAP-07), and creation judges exactly the plans and dates the job prices.
