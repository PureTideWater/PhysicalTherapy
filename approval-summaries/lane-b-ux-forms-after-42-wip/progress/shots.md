## Unexpected, or worth a look

1. **p.correspondence.error does not show the ZIP refusal (tour state, both refs).** The state fills a 7-digit phone and a 4-digit ZIP, but `packages/packets/src/correspondence.ts` (correspondenceContactSetting) throws on the first refused field, and the form schema (`settings.ts` correspondenceForm, address_zip `blankText(10, ...)`) checks only the ZIP's length. So only Phone is refused, at base and head alike; the ZIP field shows 2760 unmarked and the new words "enter five digits, or a ZIP+4 such as 27601-1234" for the correspondence ZIP appear in no shot. My base-run decision (that this id is "a refused phone and ZIP") was wrong about the app. Two follow-ups, not done here: a tour state with a valid phone and a 4-digit ZIP (say p.correspondence.error-zip) on both refs; and, for the app, whether the correspondence form should name every refused field at once like the other forms (it names one at a time; pre-existing, not this PR's diff).
2. **The correspondence ZIP hint wraps to three lines** ("Five digits, or a / ZIP+4 such as / 27601-1234.") in the narrow ZIP column at 1280 (p.correspondence.default and .error, light and dark), pushing Save the contact down 46 px. Readable, but the longest-wrapping hint on the page; a shorter hint or the hint under the whole City/State/ZIP row would read better. Minor.
3. **settings.users at 390: rows about 130 px tall with empty space.** The off-screen action column (role select, Change role, the confirm checkbox and End membership) sets each row's height, so the visible Email/Role/State columns show long blank gaps, and the State badges are cut at the edge ("activ"). This is the ScrollTable the design chose (UX PR 75's StackedRows replaces it later, design section 8), so expected, but it looks odd in the shot.
4. **The skip link at 390** covers the logo and its right edge meets the practice chooser (settings.users.skip-link-focused.390). Shown only while focused; expected for a skip link, noted for the UX review.
5. **Not this PR:** console.support.default.390 and .error.390 are 756 px wide at both refs (the grants table scrolls the page sideways at a phone width). Pre-existing; unchanged by this PR.
6. **Tour tool, fixed:** screening.no-javascript stalled 240 s per width at the head (toTop's requestAnimationFrame wait never resolves with JavaScript off). Fixed in 6c6b9ff3 on claude/build-ops (a 1 s bound) and retaken; no app defect.
