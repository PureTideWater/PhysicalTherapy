**Short answer:**

- **Pricing.** A pricing run no longer refuses a period because one of its plans has no mapping. Each line of such a plan is never priced: a line the resolver reaches is left unpriced as the existing `NO_MAPPING`, one by one (a line that fails the engine's own fact checks first is `ENGINE_ERROR`, as for any plan), and every other line prices. This takes no engine change and no new unpriced reason. The contracts resolver already gives `NO_MAPPING` for every line of an unmapped plan (`packages/contracts/src/resolver.ts:163-165`), and the engine already stops such a line (`packages/pricing/src/engine.ts:443-445`). The PR deletes the guard in three places: the run's creation (`apps/worker/src/price/create.ts:105-106`), its execution (`price/execute.ts:376-384`), and the monitor's handling of the guard's error (`monitor/plan.ts:311-313`). The months that wait on the pricing location (#33) and on Medicare content (#49, ADR 0071) keep waiting exactly as they do.
- **A period where no plan has any mapping still gets no run** (D2). That run would price nothing.
- **When a mapping queues pricing** (D2b, D20). Mapping a plan queues monitoring only when it leaves at least one month that no run covers, with every plan of its lines mapped and not held on the location or on Medicare content, while a release is in force. That monitor job carries the list of those months (dates only) and plans only them, so a month still waiting on other plans' mappings is never priced by it. So a new practice that maps all its plans gets first runs at once, with no re-run and no hand acceptance, as today, whatever order it maps them in. When months holding the plan still wait on other plans' mappings, the page says so and offers "Price them now". The daily monitor prices such mixed months anyway (the audit's rule), so nothing waits more than a day.
- **Coverage records.** A run's coverage records each plan that had no mapping when it was priced, with its line count and billed cents (`tp-pricing-coverage/2`).
- **Wording.** Coverage, Pricing runs, the home page, the coverage report and the printed audit report say "N plans have no mapping; their lines are not priced".
- **After mapping.** A new practice-wide figure says how many lines of mapped plans are still `NO_MAPPING` in the pricing in effect. The mapping list (for the practice) and each plan's page (filtered to that plan) list the runs in effect holding those lines, and offer "Price these months again", which creates #50-conforming re-runs (`createRerun`, `price/rerun.ts:220`) in that person's name, one per run in effect, and never a second one for a month already being priced again, even when two people press at once (a per-practice lock). A re-run that failed since the mapping says why and is offered again only once what it waited on has changed. Each is accepted by hand, and the list then says "triage it on Cases" until triage has judged the lines that were `NO_MAPPING` and are now priced.
- **The persisted notice and email** ("A new plan appeared: N lines, $X billed, waiting for its mapping.") need a migration (a new notification kind and role-filtered recipients) and PR #42's mail outbox and display. They go to Lane A and to a Lane B follow-up after #42. This PR ships the live notices on the screens.
- **No migration.** Postgres does not enforce MAP-01 today.

## 2. The design: price the month anyway

### 2.1 The rule

1. **A run prices every line of its period.** A line whose plan has no mapping at all is never priced: when the resolver reaches it, it is left unpriced as `NO_MAPPING`, like a line no mapping's dates cover (MAP-02). A line that fails the engine's checks before the resolver (`invalid_line_facts`, `packages/pricing/src/engine.ts:166-174,269-283`) is `ENGINE_ERROR`, as it is today for a line of any plan. No new unpriced reason; `docs/product/unpriced-reasons.md:11` ("The payer and plan have no mapping on the service date | Map the plan") already describes both.
2. **A period where no plan of its lines has any mapping gets no run** (decision D2). Such a run would price nothing. Today's behavior for these months stays: the monitor leaves the month waiting (logged), and a person's run is refused with a field error.
3. **A mapping queues pricing only when it completes a month, and prices only the complete months** (decisions D2b and D20, §4.1). Mapping a plan queues monitoring when, after the mapping and in its transaction, a release is in force and at least one month that no run covers holds this plan's lines, has every plan of its lines mapped, and is not held on the pricing location or on Medicare content (the screens' own lists of those waits, §4.1). The queued job names the months it may plan: every month no run covers that has every plan mapped and is not held, whichever plans it holds. It never plans a mixed month. Otherwise the plan's page and the mapping list say how many months wait on other plans' mappings and offer "Price them now". The daily monitor and "Price them now" plan every month no run covers, mixed months as rule 1 says, so no month waits more than a day for its mapped plans' findings. First-upload onboarding (upload, map every plan, priced) stays as it is, in any order of mappings and whatever plans each month holds: each month is priced by the first mapping that completes it, as a first run, with no re-run and no hand acceptance.
4. **Nothing is refused at execution.** The execution-time guard goes. A queued run whose plans were mapped at creation still has mappings at execution, because a mapping is corrected, never removed (`apps/worker/src/price/rerun.ts:216-217`; the mapping table grants no DELETE, `20261016160000_…sql:661`). A run created before this PR and executed after it prices as rule 1 says. `FailureCode` (`execute.ts:91`) keeps `'unmapped_plan'` for the runs that already failed with it (`failure_code` is free text with no SQL list, `20261018090000_pricing_runs.sql:43-44`). Its words are reworded (§5.4).
5. **The other waits are unchanged** (#33, #49). A month still waits, with no new run, on the pricing location (`waitingOnLocation`, `plan.ts:165`; `PricingLocationWaiting`, `create.ts:56,163`) and on Medicare content its release does not load (`contentFailure` and `coversMore`, `plan.ts:218,237`; the run fails `waiting_on_content_*` in `execute.ts:445-447`), in the monitor's order: location, content, then the mapping check of rule 2. A line of a plan with no mapping reads neither the locality nor any Medicare content (it stops at the resolver), so it never makes a mixed month fail on either. A mapping is a change of the practice's terms (`termsUnchangedSince`, `plan.ts:203`), so it releases a month's content wait, as #49 intends.

  `centsText` is signed: a line's charge has no sign CHECK in SQL (`20261016120000_remittances.sql:149`), so a sum is never forced through a non-negative schema (finding 10).
- `runCoverageSchema` (`coverage.ts:25-50`) becomes a union on `format`. `/1` parses with `unmapped_plans` defaulted to `[]`. - **Computation.** `computeCoverage(client, pricingRunId, unmappedPlanIds)` (`coverage.ts:139`; called at `execute.ts:449`). `execute.ts` passes `unmappedPlanIds(await runPlans(scope.client, lines), records.mappings)`, computed from the same `records` object the resolver priced with (`:375`). ## 4. After mapping

### 4.1 Months with no run: when a mapping queues pricing (D2b)

**The check.** A new function, `monthsAwaitingMappings(scope, practiceId)` in `apps/web/src/lib/pricing/mapped-plan.ts`:

- It takes the months `uncoveredMonths` lists (`apps/worker/src/monitor/plan.ts:117-138`, exported as it is and re-exported through `@tp/worker/pricing`, §2.2), and for each one counts the plans of its lines (under `PRICEABLE_LINE`) with and without any `payer_plan_mapping` row, and the plan IDs it holds.
- It marks each month the monitor would hold without a run whatever its mappings, from the screens' own lists of those waits, so a month shows in exactly one waiting card: `held: 'location'` for a month `monthsWaitingOnLocation` (`apps/web/src/lib/pricing/location.ts:200`, #33) lists as `zip_missing` or `location_not_ready`, and `held: 'content'` for one `monthsWaitingOnContent` (`lib/pricing/content.ts:94`, #49, ADR 0071) lists as `waiting`. A month those lists show as `repricing` or `terms_changed` is being priced again and is not held. Otherwise `held: null`.
- It returns each uncovered month as `{ periodFrom, periodTo, planIds, unmappedPlans, mappedPlans, held }`, in one query over the months' array plus those two lists, oldest first, and an empty list while no release is in force (`releaseInForce`, `location.ts:53`; the monitor prices nothing then, `plan.ts:287-290`).

**On a mapping.** `mapPlanAction` (`apps/web/src/lib/routes/mapping.ts:93-160`), when it creates a mapping (a new one or a correction, never on the `unchanged` path, `:146-148`):

0. First, before any read (review R3-6), it takes the practice's months lock: `SELECT pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended('plan_months:' || $1::uuid::text, 0))`, the codebase's own pattern (`rerun.ts:311`, `monitor/plan.ts:282`, `digest/job.ts:60`). "Price these months again" takes the same lock (§4.2). Mutations run at READ COMMITTED (`apps/web/src/lib/http/route.ts:387`), so when two people map a month's last two plans at once, the second waits for the first to commit and then sees its mapping: exactly one of the two queues the monitor. Neither `mapPlan` nor `recordRun` takes a lock of its own, so taking this one first adds no lock-order risk; it is a different key from the monitor's own (`'monitor:'`). Mappings of one practice are serialized, which costs nothing a person would notice.
1. After `mapPlan` (`:150`), in the same transaction, it calls `monthsAwaitingMappings`. The new mapping is visible there, so a month whose last run waited on content reads `terms_changed` and is not held (`termsUnchangedSince`, `plan.ts:203`, counts a mapping's `created_at`).
2. When at least one month holds this plan's lines, has `unmappedPlans = 0` and `held = null`, it registers `afterCommit(() => services.queue.enqueue('monitor', { practice_id: practiceId, request_id: request.requestId, months }))`. `months` is the `periodFrom` of **every** month `monthsAwaitingMappings` returned with `unmappedPlans = 0` and `held = null`, oldest first, up to `MONITOR_MONTHS_LIMIT` (D20, review R3-1). It is not limited to the months holding this plan: a month whose content wait the mapping released (`terms_changed`) is listed too, so #49's "being priced again" card (`content.ts:27-29`) stays true, and so is a complete month no earlier job has priced yet. A month with any plan still unmapped is never listed. This is the location save's pattern (`apps/web/src/lib/routes/settings.ts:712-720`; the job key `monitor:<practice>:<request>`, `apps/worker/src/monitor/job.ts:8-9`), queued after the commit so the plan it starts reads the new mapping. The redirect's notice is then `mapped_pricing` (or `corrected_pricing`). A mapping is never removed (§2.1 rule 4), so a month listed as complete is still complete when the job runs, however late; a month another run covered meanwhile is no longer returned by `uncoveredMonths` and is skipped.
3. Otherwise nothing is queued, and the redirect's notice is `mapped` (or `corrected`). A month held on the location or on content keeps its own card and its own step (Coverage's location and content cards), so the notice never says a month is being priced when the monitor would leave it waiting.

The monitor then plans the listed months that no run covers, each through the location, content and "nothing mapped" checks as today, and gives each a first run. It plans no other month: a mixed month is left for the daily monitor or "Price them now", and a month waiting on content that the mapping did not complete keeps waiting until then. A first run is in effect when it completes, with no acceptance, and the monitor's chain triages it. That is right here: nothing was in effect for those months, so there is no pricing for a person to compare against. A side effect, intended: a correction of a plan whose month waits on content, when every plan of that month is mapped, now queues the monitor for it, so #49's card, which says such a month "is being priced again" once the terms change (`content.ts:27-29`), is true the same minute rather than at the next daily run.

The queued `monitor` job is the practice's own work, not a person's: under SS-01's route-test rule (`expectQueuedWorkGated`, `apps/web/src/lib/routes/route-tests/define.ts` on `co-001/ss-01-suspension-refusal`), `monitor` is in `PRACTICE_OWN_JOBS`, so `mapPlanAction` stays in neither `RUN_STARTING_ROUTES` nor `CONTINUES_STARTED_WORK`, and the worker's gate refuses the job under the owner's hold (`apps/worker/src/billing/refusal.ts:14`, `RUN_STARTING_JOBS`).

**Mixed months waiting.** A month that no run covers, with at least one mapped and one unmapped plan, and `held = null`, is shown:

- On the plan's page, for months holding that plan's lines: "{N} months holding this plan’s lines wait for {P} other plans’ mappings. Map those plans first, so each month is priced once. Or price them now: the lines of plans still with no mapping stay unpriced as NO_MAPPING, and those months need pricing again once their plans are mapped. Monitoring prices them anyway at its next daily run."
- On the mapping list, for the whole practice, the same sentence with "{N} months wait for {P} plans’ mappings".
- With the button "**Price them now**" for holders of `mapping.edit`.

**"Price them now"**: a new route, `practice.mapping.price_waiting` (`apps/web/src/lib/routes/mapping.ts`), with its server action `priceWaitingFormAction` in `apps/web/src/app/p/[practiceId]/mapping/actions.ts`.

- `kind: 'action'`, `phi: true`, `permission: 'mapping.edit'`, `params: { practiceId }`, input `{ planId?: opaqueId }` (only to choose the redirect).
- `audit: { action: 'monitoring_requested', objectType: 'practice' }` (`audit_event.action` is a free regex, `20261015140500_audit_and_operations.sql:9`).
- `handle`: when a `planId` is given, load it by `(id, practice_id)` or return `NotFoundError`. Register `afterCommit` for the request-keyed `monitor` job, as above but with no `months`, so it plans every month no run covers, mixed months included, which is what the button says. Redirect to the page it came from with `notice=pricing_requested`.
- It is in `RUN_STARTING_ROUTES` (finding 8), because a person presses it only to start pricing, and the monitor it queues creates runs: a suspended practice is refused by the route wrapper with the database's reason (`apps/web/src/lib/http/invoke.ts:503-508`), and the monitor job is refused again in the worker (`apps/worker/src/billing/refusal.ts:14`). It is not in `CONTINUES_STARTED_WORK` (SS-01): it continues nothing. Being in `RUN_STARTING_ROUTES` is the safer of the two choices SS-01's rule allows for a route that queues only `monitor`: once SS-01 merges, the wrapper asks `practice_run_refusal_for` with `mapping.edit`, so a person held through a grant cannot start pricing from this button either (ADR 0080).
- The form is an `ActionForm` (#74, ADR 0077) with `<FormKeys/>`, the hidden `practiceId`, and on a plan's page the hidden `planId` with `data-error-name="This plan"`, so a refusal is named in the form's summary (the Accept form's pattern, `pricing-runs/[runId]/page.tsx:279-282`).
- Its route-test `sample` is `{ params: { practiceId }, input: {} }`: it is `ok` for every allowed persona, again and again, since queuing the request-keyed monitor changes nothing a second call depends on.
- It never creates a re-run: the monitor creates first runs only (`pricing_run_job_creates_first_runs`, `20261020090000_…sql:46`).

**The location wait, as the screens read it** (review R3-12, accepted). `held: 'location'` comes from `monthsWaitingOnLocation` (`location.ts:200-246`), which reads the newest run's `location_not_ready` alone. The monitor's own test (`waitingOnLocation`, `plan.ts:165-178`) also requires that run's cut-off to be at or after the month's newest line, and the run to postdate the last location save. So in two cases the screens hold a month the monitor would plan: lines loaded after the failed run, or a location saved since that is still not ready (a save that makes it ready reads `repricing` and is not held). The mapping then says "Mapped" and queues nothing, and the daily monitor plans the month. That run almost always fails the same way, because the lines that stopped on the location are still in the month and the status is still one of `STILL_WAITING`, so the cost is a day's delay at most, and only when the newer lines replace every line that stopped. No figure is wrong. This PR does not add the two conditions to `monthsAwaitingMappings` alone: the month would then read "being priced" on the mapping while Coverage still shows it in the location card, against the one-card rule (D18). Making the screens and the monitor agree exactly is a change to `monthsWaitingOnLocation` itself, #33's screen query, so both cards move together; it is recorded under Proposals outside this PR (6).

**Why D2b and D20** (finding 2; review R3-1): queuing the monitor on every mapping would price a new practice's months as first runs as soon as its first plan is mapped, so every later mapping would need a re-run and a hand acceptance per month (plans × months acceptances where today there are none). Queuing on completion alone is not enough either: an unrestricted monitor plans every month no run covers, so when month 1 holds plans {A, B} and month 2 holds {A, C}, mapping B (which completes month 1) would also price month 2 as a mixed first run, and mapping C would then need a re-run and a hand acceptance. The month list in the job's payload makes the job plan only the months the mapping left complete, so onboarding stays at zero acceptances in any order of mappings and any spread of plans over months, and the daily monitor keeps the audit's rule that no month freezes. Rejected: queuing only when no mixed month remains in the practice, which would hold back months already complete; a boolean "complete months only" flag that the worker works out itself, which keeps dates out of the payload but puts a second copy of the "every plan mapped" rule in the worker, where the list keeps one rule in one place (the dates are calendar months, not PHI, as the `day` and `period` payloads already are).

### 4.2 Months already priced: "Price these months again"

**The fragment.** `apps/web/src/lib/pricing/mapped-plan.ts` defines `MAPPED_NOT_PRICED(practice, lineFilter)` from the fragments `@tp/worker/pricing` already exports (`ELIGIBLE_RUN`, `PRICED_IN_EFFECT`, `RUN_IN_EFFECT`, `NEWER_RUN_PENDING`, `RERUN_ACCEPTANCE`; `public.ts:35-50`), as `lib/pricing/queries.ts` composes them: the service lines (under `lineFilter`) whose pricing in effect is `NO_MAPPING` while a mapping is now in force on their date, with the run in effect that holds each:

```sql
WITH candidate AS (                     -- lines an eligible run left NO_MAPPING (the inner filter only narrows the set)
  SELECT DISTINCT x.service_line_id
    FROM pricing_run r JOIN priced_line x ON x.pricing_run_id = r.id AND x.unpriced_reason = 'NO_MAPPING'
   WHERE r.practice_id = $1 AND ${ELIGIBLE_RUN('r')}
), in_effect AS (${PRICED_IN_EFFECT('$1', 'p.service_line_id IN (SELECT service_line_id FROM candidate)')})
SELECT p.pricing_run_id, l.id AS service_line_id, l.payer_plan_key AS plan_id, l.charge_cents,
       m.id AS mapping_id, m.created_at AS mapping_created_at
  FROM in_effect p
  JOIN service_line l ON l.id = p.service_line_id JOIN claim c ON c.id = l.claim_id
  JOIN payer_plan_mapping m ON m.id = payer_plan_mapping_in_force(l.payer_plan_key, coalesce(l.service_date, c.service_from))
 WHERE p.unpriced_reason = 'NO_MAPPING' AND (${lineFilter})
```

- The outer `p.unpriced_reason = 'NO_MAPPING'` decides, after `PRICED_IN_EFFECT` (`apps/worker/src/price/in-effect.ts:39-43`) has chosen each line's pricing in effect. Filtering inside `PRICED_IN_EFFECT` would pick an older priced line and is not done.
- `candidate` reads the practice's eligible runs (`in-effect.ts:21`) and each run's `NO_MAPPING` lines through the existing index `priced_line_by_reason (pricing_run_id, unpriced_reason)`, never a scan of the practice's priced lines (the lesson of #81, ADR 0083: every read reaches its lines through an index). No index is added, which would be a migration.
- Every query of this module is one aggregate statement that returns at most one row per run, one row per month or one row of totals, never a line (ADR 0083's bound on what a request holds), and it runs in the request's practice scope under RLS.
- **Audit** (review R3-10). The two per-run listings, `monthsToPriceAgain` and `monthsToTriage`, take the request's `scope` and read through `readWithAudit` with `{ objectType: 'pricing_run', purpose: 'payer_plan_mapping', requestId }` (the purpose every read of the mapping screens records, `mapping-queries.ts:215,288,358,461`; completeness critic, C12) and `identify: (row) => ({ objectId: row.pricing_run_id, practiceId })`: each row is a run, links to a run and is audited as one, as `readRuns` audits the Pricing runs list (`lib/pricing/queries.ts:162-169`). An event typed `priced_line` with a run's ID would be a wrong record that nothing catches (`audit_event.object_type` is a free regex and `object_id` has no foreign key, `20261015140500_audit_and_operations.sql:10-11`). The totals (`mappedNotPricedTotals`, `unmappedPlanTotals`) and `monthsAwaitingMappings` return no run or line ID, so they are unaudited aggregates, as `waitingTotals` (`mapping-queries.ts:50-58`), `monthsWaitingOnLocation` and `monthsWaitingOnContent` are.
- `payer_plan_mapping_in_force()` (`20261103090000_…sql:643-657`) mirrors `mappingInForce`, and a db test already compares the two.
- The `lineFilter` is `true` for the practice, or `l.payer_plan_key = $2` for one plan.

**Which runs.** `monthsToPriceAgain(scope, practiceId, planId | null, requestId)` groups `MAPPED_NOT_PRICED` by run: the run's period, release and status, its `lines` and `charge_cents` (bigint sum as text), and `newest_mapping_at`, the latest `mapping_created_at` among its listed lines. Ordered by `period_from, period_to, id`.

- Every such run is in effect by construction: it holds a line's pricing in effect. So #50's `not_in_effect` refusal (`rerun.ts:233-235`) cannot apply to a listed run. The action checks it again anyway, through `createRerun` (`rerun.ts:220`, unchanged since `ed85bcf3`).

**Each listed run gets one state** (findings 5 and 6; review R3-3). A re-run of the run *counts* when it was created after the run's `newest_mapping_at`, is not failed, is not accepted, and, when complete, its `RERUN_ACCEPTANCE` (`in-effect.ts:135-139`, unchanged since `ed85bcf3`) is `awaiting` or `newer_run_pending`. A re-run whose `RERUN_ACCEPTANCE` is `superseded` or `release_withdrawn` can never be accepted (`acceptRun`, `rerun.ts:305-365`), so it does not count. A re-run created after `newest_mapping_at` that **failed** is read separately: the newest such re-run, when no re-run counts, is the run's *last failed re-run*, and whether it holds the month back depends on what it waited on (the gate below). The states, in this order:

| State | When | Offered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `being_priced` | A counting re-run is queued or running | No action. "Being priced again" |
| `to_accept` | A counting re-run is complete with `RERUN_ACCEPTANCE = 'awaiting'` | A link to the re-run's page, where it is accepted by hand after its diff (J17; #50's `rerunBlock` decides whether Accept shows): "Priced again: review and accept" |
| `waiting` | The run is `NEWER_RUN_PENDING` (`in-effect.ts:123`), or a counting re-run is complete with `RERUN_ACCEPTANCE = 'newer_run_pending'` | No action. "Waits for a newer run of these dates to finish; it is listed again then, or priced by it". That newer first run reads the new mappings when it prices, so it may itself price the lines, and the list is computed again on each load |
| `rerun_failed` | The last failed re-run waited on something that has not changed since it was created (the gate below) | No action. The re-run's own label and words (below), with a link to it. Pressing again would create a re-run that fails the same way |
| `ready` | None of the above. When the run has a last failed re-run whose gate is open, the row also says why that re-run failed (below) | Included in "Price these months again" |

**The gate of a failed re-run** (review R3-3). A re-run can fail at execution although its creation passed: on the location (`location_not_ready`, `execute.ts:441-450`), on Medicare content (`waiting_on_content_*`, `:451-461`), or as `job_failed`, `release_read_outside_fetch` or `engine_version_mismatch`. A newly mapped plan's lines can read what the original run's lines never read: an MA non-contracted line has the Medicare flags set and reads MPPR and MUE where the commercial lines beside it did not. Nothing re-prices such a month by itself: the monitor plans only uncovered months, and the original's complete run still covers it. `createRerun` refuses none of this (`rerun.ts:220-251`). So the state reads the monitor's own rules for the same waits, on the re-run's period:
- **Content** (`waiting_on_content_*`): the gate opens when the practice's terms changed since the re-run was created (`termsUnchangedSince(practice, rerun.created_at)` is false, `plan.ts:205-210`) or the release in force loads more of that content for the period than the re-run's release did (`coversMore(client, { failureCode, releaseNo }, releaseInForce, period)`, `plan.ts:239`; both already exported through `@tp/worker/pricing`, `public.ts:34`).
- **Location** (`location_not_ready`): the gate opens when the practice's location was saved after the re-run was created (`practice_site.updated_at`), or the release in force is not the re-run's.
- **Every other code** (`job_failed`, `release_read_outside_fetch`, `engine_version_mismatch`): the gate is open. The operator has been alerted or the engine has changed, and pressing again is the way to price the month once it is fixed.
- While the gate is closed the state is `rerun_failed`; once it opens the month is `ready` again and its row keeps one line about the last failure. A re-run is a person's work, so the words never say the month "is priced by itself": they are this module's own, not `FAILURE_TEXT`'s (`display.tsx:80-95`, written for first runs the monitor plans again):
  - `rerun_failed`, location: label "Waiting on the billing ZIP" (`WAITING_LABEL`, `display.tsx:50-55`), then "The re-run stopped on the practice’s billing ZIP or ZIP+4. Fix it in Pricing settings; this month is offered here again once it is saved." and the link "See the re-run".
  - `rerun_failed`, content: label "Waiting on Medicare content", then "The re-run needs Medicare content PaidWhole has not loaded for these dates yet, so no line was kept. This month is offered here again once that content is loaded or a mapping or contract version changes." and the link "See the re-run".
  - `ready` after a failure: "Ready to price again. The last re-run failed: " followed by "its price job failed three times and the operator was alerted" (`job_failed`), "it read rule content it had not fetched, which is PaidWhole’s defect, and it was logged for PaidWhole’s team" (`release_read_outside_fetch`), "it was created for another version of the pricing engine" (`engine_version_mismatch`), "it waited on the billing ZIP, which has been saved since" or "it waited on Medicare content, which can be read now" (an opened gate), with the link "See the re-run".
- The server enforces the gate as the page does: a posted run whose state is `rerun_failed` is neither `ready` nor `already`, so step 3 below refuses the request as `months_changed` with nothing created.

Because the state is the run's, keyed on the newest mapping covering any of its listed lines, the practice-wide list and every plan's page show the same state for a run. A run pressed after plan B was mapped and listed again after plan C is mapped is `ready` once more (its re-run predates C's mapping and would leave C's lines `NO_MAPPING`), and pressing then creates one re-run that prices both. Pressing on two plans' pages before either re-run finishes never creates a second re-run of the same run (finding 5).

**After acceptance: "triage it on Cases"** (finding 3). A re-run a person created is never chained to triage (`apps/worker/src/price/job.ts:47-48`; `chain.ts` header; ADR 0038: "a person who works cases triages a complete run from the Cases screen"). So `monthsToTriage(scope, practiceId, planId | null, requestId)` lists, below the months to price again, each accepted re-run in effect (`rerun_of_id IS NOT NULL AND accepted_at IS NOT NULL`, and `RUN_IN_EFFECT`) that holds, as pricing in effect, a line of the listed plans (a mapped plan, or the one plan) which a mapping brought to pricing and triage would advance and has not:

- **a mapping brought it to pricing** (review R3-4): some earlier pricing of the same service line was `NO_MAPPING`, that is, `EXISTS (SELECT 1 FROM priced_line y JOIN pricing_run q ON q.id = y.pricing_run_id WHERE y.practice_id = $1 AND y.service_line_id = p.service_line_id AND y.unpriced_reason = 'NO_MAPPING' AND q.created_at < r.created_at)`, read through the existing index `priced_line_by_service_line (practice_id, service_line_id)` (`20261018090000_pricing_runs.sql:102`), a handful of rows per line. A re-run accepted after a version's sign-off or a release (`rerunReasons`, `rerun.ts:34,43-53`) whose lines were never `NO_MAPPING` is not listed: this screen did not price them again, and Cases already shows them. Any earlier pricing counts, not only the run the re-run replaced, so the chain case stays listed: re-run B (after a mapping) accepted and not yet triaged, then re-run B2 of B (after a sign-off) accepted, leaves the plan's lines untriaged and they stay on the list;

- the line is priced (`unpriced_reason IS NULL`), with a positive shortfall: `priced_line_shortfall_cents(p.id) IS NOT NULL` (`20261022090000_…sql:188-196`), the database's twin of `@tp/triage`'s `shortfallOf` (`packages/triage/src/actionable.ts:131`), which the monitoring chain already counts validated lines by (`apps/worker/src/monitor/chain.ts:39`). That is exactly the condition triage validates on (`apps/worker/src/triage/execute.ts:508-510`, "Validated: a positive shortfall … whatever else holds"; #49's `CONTENT_NOT_LOADED` reason, `actionable.ts:64,187`, holds a line from actionable, not from validated);
- it is in no `case_line` (triage's own exclusion, `execute.ts:256`);
- its newest `line_status` stage ranks below `validated` (`advanceStages`, `execute.ts:387-404`, writes only then).

Revision 2 filtered these lines in TypeScript through `shortfallOf`; the SQL function keeps the query one aggregate per run, so no line is read into the request (ADR 0083). Once triage has run on the re-run, every such line is at `validated` or beyond, and the row leaves the list. The state is `to_triage`: "Priced again and accepted: triage it on Cases", linking to `/p/<practice>/cases` for holders of `case.work`; for others, "Priced again and accepted: waits for triage by a person who works cases". The query is audited per run as above (`objectType: 'pricing_run'`, purpose `payer_plan_mapping`; review R3-10). Not auto-triaging is decision D13.

**The action.** A new route, `practice.mapping.price_again` (`apps/web/src/lib/routes/mapping.ts`), with its server action `priceAgainFormAction` in `apps/web/src/app/p/[practiceId]/mapping/actions.ts`. The mapping list's form posts no `planId` (the practice's runs); a plan page's form posts its `planId` (that plan's runs).

- Fields: `kind: 'action'`, `phi: true`, `permission: 'mapping.edit'` (the same permission as the existing re-run, `pricing.ts:325`).
- In `RUN_STARTING_ROUTES` (`apps/web/src/lib/billing/runs.ts:10-20`, finding 8): a suspended practice gets the database's reason from the route wrapper (`invoke.ts:503-508`), never a 500 from the `pricing_run_refused_while_suspended` trigger (`20261029150000_billing.sql:310`). It queues `price`, which SS-01's `expectQueuedWorkGated` counts among `PERSON_STARTED_JOBS`, so it must be in `RUN_STARTING_ROUTES` (it continues no started work, so not `CONTINUES_STARTED_WORK`). The generated route tests (`apps/web/src/lib/routes/route-tests/define.ts:147`, the suspension case) and `billing/config.test.ts:63` then cover it, unedited.
- Input, parsed by Zod:
  - `planId`: optional `opaqueId`.
  - `runIds`: hidden `runIds[]` fields joined by `apps/web/src/lib/http/form.ts`, as #50's `heldByIds` are (`pricing.ts:351-355`). Split, `opaqueId` each, at least one and at most `PRICE_AGAIN_LIMIT = 60`.
  - `releaseNo`: a positive integer, as on the re-run form.
- Audit: `{ action: 'plan_months_priced_again', objectType: 'practice' }`, with `objectId` the practice's ID. The route's audit object type is fixed per route, and one press may cover many plans; each re-run is attributed by its own event (below). The `audit_event.action` format is a free regex, so no migration is needed.
- Its route-test `sample` (every authenticated route has one, `route-tests/partition.test.ts`): `{ params: { practiceId }, input: { runIds: refs.mappedNotPricedRunId, releaseNo: '900001' } }`, a run the route-test world seeds `ready` (§8.3). The persona cases call it once per allowed persona on the same world, with no rollback between calls (`route-tests/world.ts:606`); step 3 below makes the second and later calls `ok` with nothing created, as the single re-run and Accept routes are (`acceptRun` returns an accepted run as it is, `rerun.ts:328-330`).

`handle`, in one practice-scoped transaction:

0. **Take the practice's months lock first** (review R3-6): `SELECT pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended('plan_months:' || $1::uuid::text, 0))`, the same key `mapPlanAction` takes (§4.1). `createRerun` takes no lock (`rerun.ts:215-216`, "No lock"), `NEWER_RUN_PENDING` counts first runs only (`in-effect.ts:123`), and `pricing_run_reruns` is a plain index, not a unique one (`20261019150000_…sql:100`). So without the lock, two presses at READ COMMITTED would each read every month as `ready` and each create a re-run. With it, the second press waits for the first to commit, then its step 2 sees the first's re-runs as `being_priced`, and step 3 skips them.
1. When `planId` is given, load the plan by `(id, practice_id)`. When it is missing, return `NotFoundError`. A plan of another practice is invisible under RLS anyway.
2. Compute `monthsToPriceAgain` (for the plan, or the practice): `ready` is its `ready` runs, oldest first, up to the limit, and `already` its runs in state `being_priced` or `to_accept` (a counting re-run exists since the newest mapping).
3. **Exact, with no duplicate** (decision D4, revised in revision 3, §14.2 R4): when a run of `ready` was not posted (a month became ready that the page did not show), or a posted run is in neither `ready` nor `already` (it left the list, waits for a newer run, its last re-run failed and nothing it waited on changed, was replaced, or is not the practice's), throw `InputError` on `runIds`: "the months changed since this page was shown: review them below and try again". Nothing is created (`months_changed`). A posted run in `already` is skipped: it is being priced again since its newest mapping, which is what the press asks, so no second re-run is made (finding 5). When every posted run is in `already`, nothing is created and the request succeeds. A person acts only on what the page showed them, as #50's `diff_changed`.
4. For each posted run in `ready`, oldest first:
   - Call `createRerun(scope, { practiceId, pricingRunId, releaseNo, requestId })` (#50's own function, `rerun.ts:220-251`, with its `not_found`, `not_complete`, `not_in_effect`, `newer_run_pending` and release refusals).
   - Then `services.queue.enqueue('price', { practice_id, pricing_run_id })` inside the request, as the single re-run does (`pricing.ts:341`, "a failure to queue undoes the re-run").
   - Then `recordAuditEvent(scope, { action: 'pricing_run_rerun_created', objectType: 'pricing_run', objectId: created.pricingRunId, practiceId, requestId })` (`packages/db/src/authz/audit.ts:102`). This is the same per-run event the single re-run's route records, so each re-run is attributable on its own.
5. Redirect to the page the form came from, with `notice=priced_again`. The section now shows each month as `being_priced`.

**Refusals reach the form** (finding 7). `refusal()` in `pricing.ts:300-311` is module-private and reports on `runId`, which this form does not have. The route has its own mapper, `priceAgainRefusal(error, run)`, in `mapping.ts`:

- `RerunRefusedError` → field `runIds`, naming the month by its dates: "{2026-03-01} to {2026-03-31}: {the service's message}." The service's messages are written to read after "This run:", and they read the same after the dates (for example "2026-03-01 to 2026-03-31: is not priced again yet, because a newer run of these service dates is waiting to be priced or being priced; once it finishes, re-run the run in effect."). Dates only, never PHI.
- `RuleReleaseMismatchError` → field `releaseNo`, as `pricing.ts:304-306` words it.
- `PricingLocationWaiting` (which `recordRun` throws for a re-run too, `create.ts:161-164`) → field `releaseNo` with `LOCATION_WAITING` (`pricing.ts:289`), which this PR exports for it.
- The whole request rolls back on any refusal.

**The form** is an `ActionForm` (#74, ADR 0077) with `<FormKeys/>`, the hidden `practiceId`, a hidden `runIds[]` per `ready` run with `data-error-name="The months to price again"`, the hidden `releaseNo` with `data-error-name="Rule release"`, and on a plan's page the hidden `planId` with `data-error-name="This plan"`, as #74 moved the Re-run and Accept forms' names onto their hidden fields (`pricing-runs/[runId]/page.tsx:79-80,279-282`; the old `labels` prop is gone). So a refusal names its field in the form's summary, and the focus goes to the summary. It sets `refreshOnError` to `price-again` (`apps/web/src/components/action-form.tsx:40-45,68`), as #50's Accept and Re-run forms do, so after a refusal the page is read again and the list shows the months as they are now; and `wrapSubmit`, so "Price these months again (N)" wraps at 320 px.

**Jobs queued before a rollback** (review 12f). When a later step refuses, the `price` jobs already queued for this request's re-runs stay in the queue for runs that rolled back. `priceHandler` ends each harmlessly: the run is `missing`, retried until the last attempt and then logged once as `PRICING_RUN_NOT_FOUND` with the opaque run ID (`apps/worker/src/price/job.ts:28-34`). This is the existing behavior of the single re-run and of a person's run, and no data or PHI leaks. Queuing inside the request is kept because the opposite (after commit) could leave a committed re-run with no job.

**Every re-run is created in that person's name.** `recordRun` writes `created_by` from the scope's actor (`create.ts:129-147`, the column chosen at `:135`). Postgres refuses a job's re-run (`pricing_run_job_creates_first_runs`, `20261020090000_…sql:46`), which is why this is a person's action and the monitor never re-runs.

**Accepted by hand.** Nothing here accepts. Each re-run's page lists its changes against the run it would replace and offers Accept under #50's rule (`acceptRun`, `rerun.ts:305-365`): refused when `superseded`, `newer_run_pending`, `changed_meanwhile` or `diff_changed`.

**What a re-run prices.** The original's lines, at the original's cut-off (`execute.ts:372-373`), under the release in force and the mappings, versions and settings read when it prices. Its diff therefore shows the plans' lines leaving `NO_MAPPING`. It also shows any other change since the original (another mapping, a signed-off version, a release), which the run page already explains through `rerunReasons` (`rerun.ts:43-53`, `mapping_changed` among them). That is the J17 contract and is intended. Lines loaded after the cut-off are priced by the monitor's own first runs, as today.

**Where the list sits** (decisions D5 and D14): on the mapping list for the whole practice (the place the practice-wide figure links to, and where a person who mapped several plans presses once), and on each plan's page filtered to that plan (where the redirect after mapping lands, `mapping.ts:154`). Both are the same action over the same states.

### 4.3 What the person sees

1. They map the plan. The page returns with one of:
   - `mapped_pricing` (or `corrected_pricing`): "Mapped" / "The plan is mapped from the date given. The months whose plans now all have a mapping are being priced; they show on Pricing runs when done."
   - `mapped` (or `corrected`): today's words, unchanged (§5.3). The sections below say what remains.
2. "Months waiting for a first run" (§4.1), when any, with "Price them now".
3. "Months holding this plan’s lines that are not priced" (§4.2): each month (the run's period, for example "2026-03-01 to 2026-03-31") with "N lines, $X billed, not priced" and its state.
4. When any month is `ready`, a button reads "Price these months again (N)", with the help text: "Creates a re-run of each month in your name. Nothing changes until you accept each re-run on its page, after its list of changes."
5. After pressing it, each month shows "Being priced again", then "Priced again: review and accept", linking to the re-run. A second person who presses the same page meanwhile, or at the same moment, gets the same notice and no second re-run (§4.2 steps 0 and 3). If a re-run fails, the month says why with a link to it ("Waiting on the billing ZIP" or "Waiting on Medicare content", `rerun_failed`), and is offered again only once what it waited on has changed; after any other failure it is ready again and says the last re-run failed.
6. After acceptance, the month moves to "Priced again and accepted: triage it on Cases" until triage has judged its lines.
7. A person without `mapping.edit` sees the lists and states, with no button.
