# Data dictionary

Imported verbatim from the "Data dictionary" tab of the planning document (as of 2026-10-14).

Every table, its purpose, its key fields and its policy. Conventions: `id uuid` primary key generated by the database; `created_at` and `updated_at` as `timestamptz`; money as `bigint` cents with a `_cents` suffix; rates as `numeric(14,6)`; calendar dates as `date`; every PHI table has `practice_id uuid not null` indexed, a policy on `current_setting('app.practice_ids', true)::uuid[]`, `ENABLE` and `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY`, and a row in `phi_table_registry`. Global tables have no `practice_id` and `REVOKE INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE` from the application role. Columns marked (enc) are encrypted at the application layer with the practice's data key before storage.

## Tenancy and access (PHI-adjacent; policies apply where noted)

| Table | Purpose | Key fields | Policy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `organization` | Contracting account | `kind` (group, firm, internal), `name`, `billing_customer_ref`, `status` | Members only |
| `practice` | The tenant | `organization_id` (owning group or null), `name`, `tax_id_last4`, `npi`, `locality_code`, `zip`, `settings` (jsonb: materiality, anomaly threshold, proof level, staff rate), `data_key_ref`, `status` | By grant or ownership |
| `user` | Identity reference | `cognito_sub`, `email`, `mfa_enrolled`, `hardware_key_required`, `status` | Self and administrators |
| `membership` | Role in an organization | `organization_id`, `user_id`, `role` (practice_admin, billing_manager, analyst, viewer, firm_principal, firm_analyst, platform_admin, contract_specialist, reviewer, support), `ends_at` | Organization administrators |
| `grant` | Organization access to a practice | `organization_id`, `practice_id`, `scope` (jsonb), `starts_at`, `ends_at`, `approved_by_user_id`, `agreement_id`, `revoked_at`, `revoked_by`, `reason` | Practice administrators; firm sees its own |
| `agreement` | Signed documents | `organization_id`, `practice_id` (nullable), `kind` (baa, engagement, terms, data_rights, dpa), `version`, `signed_at`, `signer`, `document_ref`, `sha256` | Parties to it |
| `support_grant` | Break-glass access | `practice_id`, `user_id`, `ticket`, `reason`, `expires_at`, `notified_at` | Platform administrators; practice sees its own |
| `phi_table_registry` | Catalog of PHI tables | `table_name`, `registered_by`, `policy_name` | Global |

## Indexes that matter

`service_line (practice_id, service_date)`, `service_line (practice_id, payer_plan_key, procedure_code)`, `claim (practice_id, payer_claim_control_number)` (on the deterministic hash of the encrypted value), `priced_line (pricing_run_id, unpriced_reason)`, `priced_line (practice_id, variance_cents desc) where variance_cents > 0`, `case (practice_id, state, deadline_on)`, `ledger_entry (practice_id, case_id, stage)`, `audit_event (practice_id, at)`, `audit_event (object_type, object_id)`, `remittance (practice_id, trn02)`, `posting_row (practice_id, check_or_trace)`, `eft_row (practice_id, trace)`.
