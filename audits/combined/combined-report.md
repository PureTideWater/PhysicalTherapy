## Summary

This merges the first audit's 64 upgrades with the second audit's 111 checked proposals (security and privacy, compliance and legal, reliability, cost, dispute success, time to value, resilience to change, AI safety, maintainability and business readiness). Duplicates were merged keeping the stronger evidence, every checker's correction was applied, a few first-audit items were split where parts land in different pull requests, and anything not truly better for the customer or the business was dropped (the main one: a success fee on recovered money, which stays a decision for you, not a build item). The result is 138 upgrades: 18 critical, 83 high, 33 medium and 4 low; 19 need an exception to the change order's freeze. The critical items are where a wrong number, a privacy failure, data loss or a launch blocker sits. Beyond the first audit's arithmetic around the pricing engine, the second audit found four more kinds: release 1 has no way into production at all and missing Medicare content is silently read as 'no limit'; Medicare Advantage packets, the lead offer, cannot be sent until each plan's real dispute route is modelled (PR 5 already holds them back, correctly); a payer's partial answer overstates 'payer approved' for good; and a single stolen administrator login or deleted key could erase every customer's data, while a restore would bring deleted practices and revoked access back. Some first-audit fixes are already being built in PR 5 and PR 6 (listed below). The most urgent single step costs an hour: pin CI's runner today, because GitHub began moving it to Ubuntu 26 on 19 October and the next run will likely fail every PR. The honest cost is about 4,750-7,590 added hours (critical 747-1,200, high 3,369-5,393, medium 618-970, low 16-27); about 3,226-5,151 of them belong before the first real audit and before production holds patient data. ### 61. Make the database, not the app, decide which practices a session can see

- **Angle:** security and privacy. **Needed:** before production holds patient data or bills a customer. **Lands in:** New PR before PR 16 (so the red team attacks it) and before PR 17 (whose multi-practice scope builds on it). **Added hours:** 90-150.
