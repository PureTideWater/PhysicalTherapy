### DD-01 should fix The email names a second factor by what the account's row says when the notice is sent: an authenticator enrolled after the reset, by whoever holds the new password, puts back the sentence R3 withholds
- Where: `packages/db/migrations/20261111090000_password_changed_notice.sql:312-324` (the claim returns `u.mfa_enrolled` as it finds it); `apps/worker/src/platform/password-notices.ts:167-173`; `packages/db/src/password-notice.db.test.ts:653-655` (a case holds it: "one enrolled between the reset and the send is told so"); `docs/adr/0091-…md:101-103` and `:255-263` (D4, D10), at ffa44ea9.
- Defect: R3 withholds "Signing in still asks for your authenticator code or hardware key." from an account with no second factor. The flag is read up to a minute after the reset (up to 24 hours while the platform service is down or rolled back). Inside a live invitation the next sign-in with the new password enrols a factor and sets the flag (`record_sign_in_with_mfa()`). D10 argues only that the flag never goes from true to false; it goes from false to true in exactly the takeover.
- Failure: SEC-03's own case, one step further. Whoever reads the invitee's mailbox resets the password (the code asked of the provider directly, while the client is public), signs in, and enrols their own authenticator before the minute's job runs. The owner's address is sent the full line. A session opened after the change is open and the authenticator is the intruder's. If the intruder stops after the reset, the owner gets the narrower line: the sentence is withheld only when the takeover did not go on. The path closes with ADR 0088's release N+1, as SEC-03's does.
- Evidence: `cd /home/user/pwnotice && REV_DB=tp_rev_d1_7 pnpm -s exec tsx <W6 scratchpad>/reviews/delta-db/scripts/exp-enrol.mts` (the worktree's `SignInFlow` with the provider twin, then the worktree's job). Case B, reset, then `sign-in with the new password: pending mfa_setup`, `enrolment … signed_in`: account `{"status":"active","mfa_enrolled":true,"notice":"pending"}`, audit trail `password_reset_completed:email_code, mfa_enrolled:totp, sign_in:totp`, sessions `[{"open":true,"opened_after_the_change":true}]`, line 2 as sent `"Every session of this account has ended. Signing in still asks for your authenticator code or hardware key."` Output: `<W6 scratchpad>/reviews/delta-db/out/q1-enrolled-after-the-reset.out`. At Cognito the first step is unproven, as it was for SEC-03.
- Fix: record the flag with the notice, in the reset's own transaction: `mfa_enrolled boolean NOT NULL` on `password_notice`, written by the trigger from the actor's row, returned by the claim in place of `u.mfa_enrolled`. Nothing clears the flag, so an account enrolled at the reset is enrolled at the send. D4 and D10 then say the address is read at send time and the flag at the reset. Tried on a scratch copy (`<W6 scratchpad>/reviews/delta-db/scripts/fix-sketch-flag-at-reset.sql`): case B is then sent the narrower line (`<W6 scratchpad>/reviews/delta-db/out/q1-fix-sketch.out`). Tests that fail before it: `password-notice.db.test.ts:653-655` with its last expectation turned round, and case B as a case of the worker's database test.
- Needs: a migration change (a column, the trigger, the claim), with the document regenerated and its approval hash.
