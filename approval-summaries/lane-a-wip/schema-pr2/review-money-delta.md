## Findings
1. note. ADR 0087 note, last bullet, says "the ledger refuses more, never less", and PR item 2 says the same. That holds only if the entries the new body leaves out each satisfy approved >= posted and approved >= deposited on their own. Then old-refuses implies new-refuses in within_line:479 (posted_T+posted_E+dP > A_T+A_E+dA together with A_E >= P_E gives P_T+dP > A_T+dA). A line moved once, written only under within_line, satisfies this. It fails for entries a two-practice scope wrote under the OLD body after a move (old body checked totals), or for superuser rows, followed by a second move back. In that state the new body accepts a posting the old one refused. Reaching it takes the owner moving a line twice and old-body history, so it is remote. Also, "counts none of that line's entries" is overstated. The new body counts the entries of the line's current practice. Fix: "counts only the entries of the line's current practice; for a line moved once the ledger refuses more, never less."
2. note. PR item 2 says "the database owner role (which only migrations use)". packages/db/src/roles.ts:4 says tp_owner "runs migrations and content loads". Fix: add "and content loads".
3. should-fix (record accuracy). PR item 1 says "All three start on an empty ledger the database has no statistics for", and ADR plan-table row 1 says "(...every measurement above)". Both contradict ADR row 4, "analyzed while empty (R's seed)", and the PR's own reviewer table ("spread R's seed is that state"). Fix: say that R's seed was analyzed while empty.
