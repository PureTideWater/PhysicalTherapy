## Findings

1. blocking (by the brief's rule "breakable by a non-superuser"; in practice limited to the migration/owner role). The invariant in equivalence.md section 1 item 2 does not hold for tp_owner. service_line has no guard against an UPDATE of practice_id (its only triggers: service_line_updated_at BEFORE UPDATE, service_line_references_in_practice BEFORE INSERT). tp_owner's UPDATE passed RLS and triggers and failed only on the FK to a practice that was absent from the copy. tp_app is refused (`permission denied for table service_line`). After such an UPDATE, the new ledger_line_net returns 0 for the line's existing entries where the old body summed them. ledger_entry_within_line then refuses postings the old body accepted, so the failure is closed. Smallest fix: change equivalence.md 1.2(c)/(e) and the draft's comment at line 61 to "every role but a superuser or tp_owner (migrations, which never update service_line.practice_id)", and get the founder's ruling on that scope. The structural fix, a BEFORE UPDATE guard on service_line.practice_id, needs its own grant because it is a new trigger.
2. should-fix (purpose, not correctness). The plan claim fails on a large never-analyzed ledger. With 20,000 entries the installed new body plans `ledger_entry_by_date` with `Filter: (service_line_id = $1)` and a Sort, in both generic and custom plans, while the old body plans ledger_entry_by_line. Draft line 71 ("whatever it knows of the ledger") is false. The worker plan test passes only because its never-analyzed ledger is small. Smallest fix: narrow the claim on line 71 and in adr 0087 to the measured states (empty or small never-analyzed, or analyzed), and add a plan case with a few thousand never-analyzed entries. If the purpose needs to hold there, reconsider the shape of the body (only with the founder, since the body is fixed by the grant).
3. note. After ANALYZE of an empty ledger, every lookup plans a Seq Scan, so "uses ledger_entry_by_line" is not unconditional even for the new body. That costs nothing while the table is empty.
4. note. readsOf finds only the exact spelling `FROM ledger_match`. A third read spelled `FROM public.ledger_match` or `JOIN ledger_match` would escape the length-2 guard. Smallest fix: count reads with `\b(FROM|JOIN)\s+(public\.)?ledger_match\b`.
5. note. The draft is not yet in packages/db/migrations. The PR must also regenerate packages/db/functions/ledger_line_net.sql and ledger_entry_rules.sql (body hashes 5d0a94c0a294a732 and 834cdefc070b08db). If either file is unchanged, the function was changed without being regenerated.
