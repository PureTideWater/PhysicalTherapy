### UX-01 should fix The email's own words make the natural answer to it unreadable at the support desk, and the new playbook does not say so
- Where: `packages/auth-mail/src/words.ts:139` and `:142` (the time and the support address the email holds); `apps/worker/src/support/support-mail.ts:54-57` and `:78-80`; `packages/observability/src/phi-guard.ts:44-46`; `docs/runbooks/support-mail.md:39-40` and `:59-71`; `docs/adr/0091-…md:302-304`.
- Defect: the only route out is "write to <support address> from this address". The support inbox withholds any message whose text holds a date, an email address or a phone number (`writtenPhiFindings`). The email's first line holds a date and its last an address, so a reply that quotes it, a forward of it, its text pasted, or a new message that repeats the time it gave each opens a ticket that reads only "Mail to support, withheld", whose fixed text tells the desk to "Ask them to write again through the in-app form". The new playbook "Password changed, not by me" assumes the desk can read what the person wrote and never mentions a withheld ticket; ADR 0091's Consequences say the sentence "brings them to the support desk's 'Password changed, not by me'". Rule: every error says what happened and a next step the reader's role can take (`PRD.md:256`; baseline); a hand-off carries its record (baseline).
- Failure: a person whose password was changed by someone else forwards the email to support with "this was not me". The desk sees a withheld ticket from that account, cannot tell it from any other withheld mail, and answers as the ticket says: use the in-app form, which needs the sign-in the person has lost. The mailbox-compromise steps start a round of mail later, or never.
- Evidence: ran `pnpm -s exec tsx S/screen-reply.ts` from the worktree: the worker's own `readMail` and `screenMail` on synthetic, DMARC-passing messages from the account's address (`S/screen-reply.out`):

      1 new message, no detail: verdict=ticket
      2 new message naming the time the email gave: verdict=withheld   ["the text holds a calendar date (a date of birth shape)"]
      3 reply quoting the notice: verdict=withheld                     [date, email address, date written in words]
      4 forward of the notice: verdict=withheld                        [date, email address]
      5 the notice text alone, pasted: verdict=withheld                [date, email address]
      6 new message with a signature phone number: verdict=withheld    ["the text holds a phone number shape"]

  Line 1 of the notice alone is "a calendar date"; line 4 alone is "an email address". The screen is the base's (ADR 0055); this change's own are the email that points at it and the playbook.
- Fix: (1) the playbook, in this pull request. In `docs/runbooks/support-mail.md`, before step 1 of "Password changed, not by me", add: "A reply to that email, a forward of it, or a message that repeats the time it gave is withheld (`SUPPORT_MAIL_WITHHELD`; the ticket reads "Mail to support, withheld"): the email's own first line holds a date and its last an address, which the screen takes for patient information, as it takes a signature's phone number. Treat a withheld ticket from a person who cannot sign in as this case until they say otherwise: answer by email, ask whether their password was changed without them, and never send them to the in-app form, which needs the sign-in they may not have." At `:39-40` add: "A person who cannot sign in cannot use the form: see "Password changed, not by me"." In ADR 0091:303-304 write: "…brings them to the support desk's "Password changed, not by me" when the message opens a readable ticket; a reply or a forward is withheld, and the playbook says how the desk treats it."
  (2) The best option, in the worker: when a withheld message's subject, less any "Re:" or "Fwd:", is the notice's own ("Your password was changed | PaidWhole"), `screenMail` gives the ticket the fixed subject "Mail to support, withheld: an answer to “Your password was changed”" (a fact code computes from a fixed string, never the message's text); and the notice goes with Reply-To the support address, as the screening's mail does (`apps/worker/src/mail/mail.ts:136`, `:171`), so a plain reply from the account's mailbox reaches the desk and is named. Tests that fail before it: a case in `apps/worker/src/support/support-mail.db.test.ts` (a quoted reply opens a ticket whose subject names the notice) and one in `apps/worker/src/platform/password-notices.test.ts` (the reply address).
- Needs: nothing for (1). (2) is a worker change outside this diff's 63 files; no migration.

### UX-02 should fix For three kinds of reader the route out ends in silence, and the email names no other
- Where: `packages/auth-mail/src/words.ts:142`; `apps/worker/src/support/mime.ts:272-301`; `packages/db/functions/operator_record_support_mail.sql:37`; `docs/adr/0091-…md:89-92` (D4); `docs/runbooks/support-mail.md:32-36`.
- Defect: "write to <support address> from this address now, and we will help you secure the account" promises help through an inbox that opens a ticket only when SES verified the sender (a DMARC pass for the From domain, or a DKIM signature aligned with it) and the From address is an account's that is not disabled. Anything else is recorded by a code and nobody answers (the receipt rule only stores the message, `infra/lib/constructs/support-mail.ts:76-82`). Three readers of this email fall there: (a) one whose practice domain has no DMARC record and no DKIM key of its own (`unverified_sender`); (b) one whose account was disabled after the reset, whom D4 chooses to tell (`unknown_sender`); (c) one whose mail is forwarded or aliased, who reads "this address" as the mailbox they are in, where the screens say "the address you sign in with" (`apps/web/src/app/sign-in/notice.tsx:34`, `:44`, `:181`) (`unknown_sender`). Rule: the next step is said, never a dead end (`PRD.md:256`, `:260`); one name per thing across screens (recommendation) for (c).
- Failure: the reader does exactly what the email says and hears nothing; the desk never learns who wrote. The in-app form, which the runbook offers such senders (`support-mail.md:32-36`), needs the sign-in they have lost.
- Evidence: `pnpm -s exec tsx S/screen-sender.ts` (`S/screen-sender.out`), a clean message from the account's address:

      DMARC passes: verdict=ticket
      no DMARC record, the mail host signs with its own domain, SPF passes: verdict=unverified_sender
      no DMARC record, no signature, SPF passes: verdict=unverified_sender

  In a copy of `tp_pwn_tpl` (`S/checks.sql` part 3, `S/disabled-route.out`), as `tp_operator`: with the account active the message is `opened | t`; after `UPDATE "user" SET status = 'disabled'`, `operator_password_notice_claim()` still returns the address (the notice is sent) and the same message is `unknown_sender | f`. The sender check and the disabled rule are the base's; the promise, D4's choice and "this address" are this email's.
- Fix: a second route that needs no inbound mail, in the screens' own words. Replace line 4 (`words.ts:142`) with: `If you did not, write to ${supportEmail} now from the address you sign in with, and tell your organization’s administrator. We will help you secure the account.` The administrator can reach the desk through the in-app form and end the person's membership in Settings, and the playbook already works "with the organization's administrator". Add to the playbook's lead (`support-mail.md:59-62`): "The email also asks the person to tell their organization's administrator, who may be the one who writes." The test that pins the line and fails first: `packages/auth-mail/src/render.test.ts:233-238`.
- Needs: a ruling of the words' owner (#125's lines were ruled by Lane A's orchestrator as the brand owner's delegate). No migration.
