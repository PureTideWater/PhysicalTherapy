# NNNN. Title in the imperative: what was decided

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** names or roles
- **PRD sections:** the sections of docs/product/PRD.md this decision implements or interprets

## Context

What forces are at play: the requirement, the constraint, the risk. State facts, cite the PRD section or
the source, and say what happens if nothing is decided.

## Decision

What we will do, in one or two paragraphs. Specific enough that a reviewer can tell whether code follows it.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why not (or when we would switch) |
| --- | --- |
| The option | The reason, and the trigger that would make us revisit it |

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we must now test or monitor, and what a later milestone
inherits. Include the check that proves the decision holds, if there is one.

## Revisit when

The event, milestone or measurement that would reopen this decision.
